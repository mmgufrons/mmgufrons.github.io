Portofolio digital interaktif Maulana Gufron yang merangkum rekam jejak profesional di dua bidang utama: **Business & AI Operations** (arsitektur sistem & tools operasional) dan **Creative & Visual Design** (identitas brand, packaging, & grafis).

🌐 **Akses Live:** [https://mmgufrons.github.io/](https://mmgufrons.github.io/)

---

## 📌 Ringkasan Ekosistem & Modul

Portofolio ini mengintegrasikan seluruh purwarupa operasional dalam satu wadah (*Unified Hub*):

- **[Marketing Operations Suite (MKT.OS)](https://mmgufrons.github.io/mkt/index.html)**: 7 tools operasional tim Marketing & CS (Admin Panel, B2B Partner Board, Mitra Canvassing Tracker, Campaign Leads Tracker, PPOB Dashboard, Marketplace Report, & Legal Document System).
- **[SMA Enterprise ERP](https://mmgufrons.github.io/sma/index.html)**: Workstation koordinasi lintas divisi, manajemen tugas harian, dan direktori tools internal.
- **[SiCek Ekspedisi](https://mmgufrons.github.io/cekongkir/index.html)**: Kalkulator ongkos kirim multi-ekspedisi dan pelacak resi.
- **[Galeri Desain Klasik](https://mmgufrons.github.io/desain/index.html)**: 12 studi kasus identitas brand, desain kemasan, dan visual branding.
- **Portal Pendukung**: [Dokumentasi API B2B](https://mmgufrons.github.io/apidocs/), [Dynamic QRIS Soundbox](https://mmgufrons.github.io/lpqris/), dan [Portal SOP Panduan](https://mmgufrons.github.io/panduan/).

## 🛠️ Catatan Teknis Singkat
- **Platform:** GitHub Pages (Static Serverless).
- **Teknologi:** Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5 & Tailwind CSS.
- **Bilingual:** Engine alih bahasa client-side instan (`assets/js/i18n.js`) tanpa reload.
- **Aksesibilitas:** Rasio kontras tinggi standar WCAG AAA.
